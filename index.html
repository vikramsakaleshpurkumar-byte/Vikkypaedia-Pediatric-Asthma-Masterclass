Why This Module Exists: Ending SABA Monotherapy & The Indian 'Oral Syrup' Epidemic

GINA 2026 [Ref 1, 50] and BTS/NICE/SIGN NG245 [Ref 3] enacted the most important transformation in 30 years of respiratory medicine: eliminating SABA-only treatment across all ages in favor of Anti-Inflammatory Reliever (ICS-formoterol AIR & MART) in adolescents (≥12y) and concomitant/daily ICS in children (6–11y).

Yet in India (**GAN Phase I Study [Ref 46]**), fewer than 25% of asthmatic children receive daily inhaled corticosteroids while millions are prescribed ineffective **oral salbutamol syrups** and unnecessary **antibiotics** for viral wheeze. Simultaneously, Indian clinicians must distinguish true asthma from **Tropical Pulmonary Eosinophilia (TPE) [Ref 11]**, **Endobronchial TB [Ref 9]**, **Foreign Body Aspiration**, and **ABPA [Ref 47]**.

Zero Hallucinations Every cutoff (FEV₁/FVC <0.90, BDR ≥12%, FeNO ≥25 ppb, IV MgSO₄ 40–50 mg/kg, DEC 6 mg/kg/d) is linked onsite to its exact primary publication.
Diagnostic & Therapeutic Stewardship Explicitly teaches what NOT to do (no SABA-only therapy, no oral salbutamol syrups, no sedatives in acute wheeze, no routine antibiotics or CXRs).

Track 1 (≥12y): ICS-formoterol AIR (Steps 1–2) & MART (Steps 3–5). 6–11y: ICS whenever SABA taken (Step 1), Daily ICS (Step 2), MART (Steps 3–4) Cuts severe exacerbations by 55–64% (SYGMA / Novel START) while lowering cumulative steroid load
ERS Pediatric Diagnosis 2021 [Ref 2] Children 5–16 years Requires ≥2 objective tests: Spirometry (FEV₁/FVC <LLN or <0.80–0.90), BDR ≥12%, and FeNO ≥25 ppb Prevents both misdiagnosis of non-asthmatic cough and under-diagnosis of reversible airflow obstruction
BTS / NICE / SIGN NG245 (2024) [Ref 3] Children, young people & adults Unified UK algorithm: Eosinophils/FeNO/BDR confirmation + immediate AIR / low-dose MART initiation Harmonizes NICE biomarker stewardship with BTS/SIGN acute and chronic asthma pathways
India NHM IMNCI (2023) [Ref 5] Infants & children 2m–5y at PHC/CHC/District level Fast breathing / chest indrawing + audible wheeze → 3 cycles of inhaled Salbutamol (20 min apart) BEFORE classifying Pneumonia Prevents massive antibiotic misuse in viral wheeze and acute asthma across Indian primary care
IAP ATM & Acute Consensus [Ref 8, 9] Indian children & adolescents Bans oral salbutamol syrups; mandates pMDI + valved spacer (or Zar 500-mL bottle spacer [Ref 10]); O₂ 92–95% (GINA 2026 target for 6–17y; >=92% for <=5y) + SABA/Ipratropium + Steroid + IV MgSO₄ Resource-adapted Indian formulary dosing, spacer care, and environmental trigger control (biomass/coils)
