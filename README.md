# Vikkypaedia — Pediatric Asthma & Acute Wheeze Masterclass

An evidence-based, interactive medical education masterclass on pediatric asthma and acute wheezing disorders.

## Core Pedagogical & Clinical Architecture
- **Global + Indian Harmonization**: Built on the **GINA 2026 Strategy Report** (Track 1 MART / AIR vs Track 2), BTS / NICE / SIGN NG245 (2024), ERS/ATS technical standards, and Indian guidelines (IAP Standard Treatment Guidelines, NHM IMNCI / F-IMNCI 2023, and ICMR Antimicrobial Stewardship).
- **Age-Specific Pathways**:
  - Children &lt;5 Years (preschool wheeze phenotypes, PRAM score, modified API, step-wise controller therapy).
  - Children 6–11 Years (diagnosis, spirometry, bronchodilator reversibility, low/medium/high ICS-LABA dosing).
  - Adolescents 12+ Years (GINA Track 1 as-needed and maintenance ICS-Formoterol).
  - Severe Asthma &amp; Biologics (Omalizumab, Mepolizumab, Dupilumab, Tezepelumab).
- **50 Verified Non-Hallucinated Citations**: Every threshold, drug dose, and algorithm is mapped to real Vancouver citations cross-checked against NCBI PubMed.
- **Interactive Virtual ER Simulator**: 3 unfolding cases (acute severe status asthmaticus, preschool multi-trigger wheeze, and severe refractory asthma workup) with real-time digital monitor, bedside blood gases, and PRAM scoring.
- **110 Board-Style Tiered MCQs**: 10 questions per unit with track filtering (L1 Nurse, L2 UG, L3 PG), spaced recall flagging, and cited rationales.
- **Offline-First & Zero Tracking**: Runs completely in the browser; learner progress is stored locally in `localStorage`.
